# First 100 steps

What the bot does at each of the first 100 steps of the B0aty HCIM Guide V3, as reviewed by hand. `FirstHundredTest` checks the bot still plans every step this way.

**Runs** means the bot does the step on its own. **Pauses** means it stops, shows the step, and waits for you to do it and tick it in the plugin.

Totals: 61 run, 39 pause.
